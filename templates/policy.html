Your information matters

MoviPilot is designed to collect only the information reasonably needed to provide, secure and improve the service.

01

Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MoviPilot collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use our website and services.

MoviPilot is committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MoviPilot, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Account credentials.
  • Account creation date and related account information.

Authentication Information

If you use third-party authentication such as Google sign-in, we may receive information provided by the authentication provider, such as your name, email address and other information necessary to establish your account.

Security Information

MoviPilot may temporarily process information associated with password recovery and account verification, such as verification codes, expiration information and related security records.

We do not store your password as plain text.

Account passwords are processed using password hashing mechanisms rather than being stored in their original readable form.

03

How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and maintain your MoviPilot account.
  • Authenticate you securely.
  • Help you recover your account.
  • Send account-related emails.
  • Provide movie discovery and recommendation features.
  • Maintain and improve the platform.
  • Detect, investigate and prevent abuse, fraud or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
04

Password and Authentication Security

Passwords are not intended to be stored in readable plain-text form. MoviPilot uses password hashing to protect account credentials.

Password-reset verification codes are also handled as security-sensitive information and are not intended to be stored as readable verification codes.

You should never share your password or verification code with another person.

05

Account and Service Emails

MoviPilot may send emails necessary for the operation of your account, including:

  • Account verification messages.
  • Password-reset verification codes.
  • Security-related notifications.
  • Welcome and account-related messages.

These messages are considered part of the service and may be sent even where promotional communications are not enabled.

06

Third-Party Services

MoviPilot may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These may include movie information providers, authentication providers, email delivery services, hosting providers, analytics tools and other infrastructure providers.

Where third-party services process information on our behalf, their processing may also be governed by their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

07

Cookies and Similar Technologies

MoviPilot may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain authentication, security, preferences and other functionality.

For example, authentication and password-reset flows may use security cookies to maintain a user's session or verification state.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect functionality that depends on them.

08

When Information May Be Shared

MoviPilot does not intend to sell your personal information as a commercial product.

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the platform.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or lawful governmental requests.
  • Protect the rights, security or property of MoviPilot, users or other persons.
  • Investigate suspected fraud, abuse or security incidents.
09

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account security, satisfy legitimate operational requirements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Security information such as password-reset verification records may have shorter retention periods because they are designed for temporary authentication purposes.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing.

You may also have rights relating to how your information is processed and to obtain information about processing activities.

Requests should be made through the official MoviPilot privacy or support contact channel.

i
Privacy requests

11

Data Security

MoviPilot takes reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Security measures may include password hashing, access controls, secure authentication flows, temporary verification mechanisms and appropriate safeguards for stored information.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their own accounts and credentials.

12

Children's Privacy

MoviPilot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where doing so would violate applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to MoviPilot in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us.

13

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, data practices or legal obligations change.

The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated effective date.

14

Contact Us

If you have a privacy question, want to make a privacy request, or believe your information has been handled incorrectly, contact MoviPilot through the official privacy or support channel.
